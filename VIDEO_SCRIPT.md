# KhetKit — 3-minute demo video script (target 2:40)
Voice: Akshay (or captions only). Screen: https://khetkit.sites.tab.bot/ at 1440x900.

0:00–0:15 — Hook. Title card "KhetKit". VO: "A farmer in Maharashtra sees tomato leaves curling. The nearest shop sells him a pesticide and a dose that may be wrong. KhetKit fixes the first step: buying the right thing, safely, and letting anyone pay for it."
0:15–0:45 — Need → kit. Click the "Tomato leaf curl" example, Build the kit. VO: "An AI procurement agent reads the need and picks items only from a vetted catalogue, sized to one acre and a 60-dollar budget. Every line says why, in plain words." Highlight model + milliseconds line.
0:45–1:05 — Hindi. Click the Hindi example. VO: "Same in Hindi: the kit and every reason come back in Hindi. For a fruit borer it picks pheromone traps, not a spray."
1:05–1:35 — Safety floor. Click "Wants a pesticide dose". VO: "Ask for an imidacloprid dose and KhetKit refuses to dose; it adds a KVK expert visit instead." Then type "my brother sprayed and is vomiting". VO: "Poisoning words get emergency numbers and no kit. These rules run in code, after the model, and they are unit-tested."
1:35–2:15 — PayPal. Back to the tomato kit. VO: "Now a supporter, say a relative in Dubai, funds it." Choose $25, PayPal button, sandbox login, approve. Show funding bar at 50%. Pay the rest. VO: "Each kit line is a PayPal line item; partial funding is tracked; with our sandbox REST app the Worker creates and captures the order itself."
2:15–2:35 — Fulfilment. Show receipt + plan. VO: "Funded, the agent groups the lines by supplier — KVK, seed dealer, FPO shop — and queues supplier payouts for delivery confirmation." Show public ledger.
2:35–2:40 — Close. "KhetKit. Built by Tab, an AI assistant, for team lead Akshay Wagh. Code on GitHub, MIT."
